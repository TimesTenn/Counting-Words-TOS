# Privacy Policy — Counting Words **Last Updated:** October 14, 2026 ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how Counting Words processes information when users interact with it on Discord. By using Counting Words, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this policy. ## 2. Information Processed Counting Words' processes the following information as necessary to provide its word-counting functionality: - **Message content:** Counting Words reads accessible channel message history to calculate word counts. - **Discord messages:** Counting Words checks message content to count words and skips messages sent by bots or messages with empty content. - **Word-count results:** Counting Words calculates the total number of words and messages checked and returns the results to the user who runs the command. - **Discord permissions:** Counting Words relies on its channel access permissions to determine which messages it can read. Counting Words does not need to collect personal information such as real names, email addresses, or physical addresses to perform its word-counting function. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information accessed by Counting Words is used solely to provide its word-counting functionality. When a user runs the `/wordcount` command, Counting Words scans the accessible message history of the relevant channel, calculates the word and message counts, and returns the results. The results are sent as an ephemeral Discord response, meaning they are intended to be visible only to the user who invoked the command. ## 4. Data Storage and Retention Counting Words recalculates word counts from accessible message history whenever the command is run, the command may take a moment to run if there is a large message history in the channel. As currently stated, Counting Words does not maintain a database of message content or persist word-count totals between command uses. Message content is processed to calculate the requested results. This does not mean that Discord itself does not retain messages under its own policies. ## 5. Sharing of Information Counting Words' operator does not intentionally record, sell, or share message content with third parties. Counting Words operates through Discord's platform and API. Information may therefore be processed by Discord in accordance with Discord's own policies and applicable terms. ## 6. Security Reasonable steps should be taken to protect Counting Words' credentials and prevent unauthorized access to its code and configuration. Counting Words' Discord token is stored in a private environment configuration file and should never be published or shared. However, no method of electronic processing or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 7. User Rights and Choices Users can choose not to use Counting Words word-counting functionality with no effect to the channel they are typing in. Server administrators can manage Counting Words' channel permissions and restrict its access to message history. Users may also contact the Bot's operator with questions or concerns about privacy. ## 8. User Privacy Counting Words is intended to operate within Discord's platform and is not designed to collect personal information from any account or profile. Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies to use this bot. ## 9. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as Counting Words' features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. ## 10.
